# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Be direct, respectful, and constructive. Discuss the work, not the person. Respect privacy, different experience levels, and reasonable disagreement.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not accepted.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project spaces. Report conduct problems privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Include links and relevant context, but do not investigate or retaliate yourself.

Maintainers will review reports privately and may edit or remove content, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Knowingly false or bad-faith reports may receive the same response.
